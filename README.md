# IBM HR Attrition Analysis

## About

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and identify the workforce segments and employee characteristics associated with employee turnover.

The analysis explores attrition across departments, job roles, overtime, age groups, salary levels, business travel, marital status, education, and other employee attributes.

## Tech Stack

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query

## Key Insights

- **Younger employees show the highest attrition**, with the 18–25 age group reaching a 35.8% attrition rate, highlighting early-career retention as a key area of concern.

- **Overtime is strongly associated with attrition**, with employees working overtime showing a 30.5% attrition rate versus 10.4% for those who do not.

- **Sales represents the highest-risk department**, with a 20.6% attrition rate, while Sales Representatives reach 39.8%.

- **Lower salary bands show substantially higher attrition**, with employees earning under 3K recording a 28.6% attrition rate compared with 3.8% among employees earning 15K+.

- **Frequent business travel is linked with higher attrition**, with a 24.9% attrition rate among frequent travellers compared with 8.0% among non-travellers.

> **Note:** The findings represent associations observed within the dataset and do not establish causation.
